Data integrity. Every number on this page comes from the CSV files you imported; nothing is estimated or filled in. Gaps mean no data. Percent change is not shown when the earlier month is 0. Viewers (Facebook) and Reach (Instagram) are daily unique counts. Adding days together would count the same people more than once, so these always show as an average per day. Facebook and Instagram are never added together. Colours show the direction of a change, not whether it is good or bad, unless you set that per metric with the ⓘ button. All processing happens in this browser, and your data is stored locally (IndexedDB).
